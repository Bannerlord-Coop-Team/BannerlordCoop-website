# Code of Conduct

## Our Community

Bannerlord Coop is an **open-source community project** dedicated to bringing a cooperative campaign experience to Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord.

The project is developed through contributions from developers, testers, designers, translators, players, and other community members. Anyone interested in improving the project is welcome to participate, regardless of experience level.

Open-source collaboration depends on people being able to discuss ideas, review each other's work, disagree constructively, and contribute without unnecessary hostility. We therefore want our development and community spaces to remain welcoming, productive, and respectful.

Participation in the Bannerlord Coop project or its official community spaces means agreeing to follow this Code of Conduct.

## Expected Behavior

We expect community members to:

* Treat others with respect, even when you disagree.
* Give constructive feedback and focus criticism on ideas, code, designs, or decisions rather than individuals.
* Be patient with contributors and players of different experience levels.
* Welcome new contributors and help them understand the project when reasonably possible.
* Respect project maintainers, moderators, contributors, and their decisions.
* Keep discussions relevant and avoid deliberately disrupting conversations.
* Accept constructive criticism and take responsibility for mistakes.
* Respect people's privacy and personal boundaries.
* Help maintain a collaborative environment where people feel comfortable contributing.

Technical disagreements are normal in an open-source project. Debate, alternative implementations, and criticism are welcome, but personal attacks are not.

## Open-Source Collaboration

Bannerlord Coop is developed publicly and relies on voluntary contributions from the community.

Contributing code, documentation, testing, bug reports, translations, designs, suggestions, or other work does not guarantee that a contribution will be accepted or merged.

Maintainers may reject or request changes to contributions based on factors such as:

* Project direction
* Code quality
* Maintainability
* Compatibility
* Performance
* Security
* Scope
* Technical architecture

A rejected pull request or suggestion is not a personal rejection of the contributor.

Contributors are encouraged to discuss significant changes with the development team before investing substantial time into an implementation.

Nobody is entitled to another contributor's time. Maintainers and contributors should not be pressured to review code, implement features, fix issues, or provide release dates.

Constructive disagreement about the direction of the project is welcome. Harassment or hostility toward maintainers or contributors because of project decisions is not.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable within Bannerlord Coop community spaces:

* Harassment, intimidation, bullying, or targeted abuse.
* Personal insults or repeated hostile behavior.
* Discrimination or derogatory comments directed at a person's identity or background.
* Threats of violence or encouraging harm toward another person.
* Sharing another person's private or identifying information without permission.
* Unwanted sexual attention or inappropriate sexually explicit content.
* Trolling, excessive spam, deliberate disruption, or repeatedly provoking other members.
* Impersonating developers, moderators, contributors, or other community members.
* Continuing unwanted contact after someone has clearly asked you to stop.
* Encouraging or organizing harassment against members of this or another community.
* Knowingly submitting malicious code, malware, credential stealers, backdoors, or other content intended to harm users, contributors, or project infrastructure.
* Deliberately abusing project infrastructure, repositories, CI systems, servers, or other services.

Disagreement with someone's implementation, pull request, bug report, design decision, or opinion does not justify abusive behavior.

## Project Discussions and Contributions

When participating in issues, pull requests, code reviews, development discussions, or testing:

* Keep feedback relevant to the work being discussed.
* Explain technical disagreements where possible instead of dismissing someone's work.
* Review contributions based on their technical merits rather than the person submitting them.
* Avoid demanding work from volunteer contributors.
* Do not repeatedly pressure maintainers to merge a contribution, implement a feature, fix an issue, or provide a release date.
* Do not intentionally derail issues or pull requests with unrelated arguments.
* Respect review decisions, even if your contribution is not accepted.
* Do not use bug reports, pull requests, or other project tools to attack or publicly shame another person.
* Be willing to revise contributions in response to reasonable technical feedback.

Maintainers may close, lock, hide, or remove discussions that are no longer productive.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to official Bannerlord Coop spaces, including:

* GitHub repositories
* Issues and pull requests
* Code reviews and development discussions
* Official Discord channels
* Testing and playtesting environments
* Project-managed servers and services
* Other official Bannerlord Coop community spaces

It may also apply when someone is publicly representing Bannerlord Coop or acting on behalf of the project.

## Reporting Problems

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it privately by opening a ticket through the ticket system in the [official Bannerlord Coop Discord server](https://discord.gg/bannerlordcoop).

When possible, include relevant information such as:

* What happened
* Where it happened
* Who was involved
* Links, screenshots, or other relevant context

Do not create a public GitHub issue containing sensitive information about a harassment report.

Reports will be reviewed by the project team as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible. Information about a report should only be shared when necessary to investigate or resolve the situation.

If a report involves a particular maintainer or moderator, it should be handled by another member of the project team whenever possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers and moderators are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the circumstances and severity of the behavior, actions may include:

1. **Informal Correction**
   A private or public request to stop inappropriate behavior.

2. **Formal Warning**
   A warning explaining the violation and the behavior expected going forward.

3. **Temporary Restriction**
   Temporary removal from discussions, development spaces, Discord, testing environments, or other project services.

4. **Permanent Removal**
   Permanent removal from Bannerlord Coop community or development spaces.

Severe behavior may result in immediate removal without prior warning.

Attempts to evade moderation actions through alternate accounts or other means may result in additional restrictions.

## Moderator Responsibilities

Maintainers and moderators are expected to apply these rules reasonably and consistently.

Moderation decisions should be based on behavior rather than someone's status, contribution history, technical ability, or relationship with members of the project team.

Moderators may remove, edit, reject, or lock comments, commits, issues, pull requests, discussions, or other contributions when they violate this Code of Conduct or significantly disrupt the project.

## Good Faith

Not every disagreement or uncomfortable interaction is a Code of Conduct violation.

Open-source development naturally involves differing opinions about architecture, implementation, priorities, and project direction.

Community members are encouraged to assume good faith when reasonable, communicate clearly, and attempt to resolve ordinary misunderstandings respectfully.

At the same time, repeated behavior may become unacceptable even when individual incidents appear minor.

## Final Note

Bannerlord Coop exists because people voluntarily contribute their time, knowledge, and effort to an open-source project.

Code, testing, bug reports, documentation, translations, design work, suggestions, and community support all contribute to the project's development.

Respect the people behind those contributions, respect the community, and help keep Bannerlord Coop a project where people want to participate.
